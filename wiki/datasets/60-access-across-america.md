**Category:** Jobs & mobility

**Data source:** Access Across America

**Provider / steward:** University of Minnesota Accessibility Observatory / Center for Transportation Studies

**Provider type:** University research / public data

**Geographic coverage:** United States; metro summaries focus on major metropolitan areas

**Granularity:** Census block access measures; metro summaries

**Access / format:** Public reports and data via University Digital Conservancy

**Typical update:** Annual series; 2024 data year available

**Primary hackathon uses:** Compare access to jobs by auto, transit, biking, and walking; identify housing locations with strong multimodal opportunity access

**Relevant problem briefs:** Typology & Climate; Observatory; Feasibility

**Likely join key:** Census block GEOID; metro / year

**Caveats / licensing notes:** Mode, travel-time threshold, coverage and data availability vary by year/product. Verify the specific download and reuse terms in the Digital Conservancy; accessibility is modeled, not observed commutes.
