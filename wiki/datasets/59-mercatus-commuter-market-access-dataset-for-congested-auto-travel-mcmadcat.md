# Mercatus Commuter Market Access Dataset for Congested Auto Travel (McMADCAT)

Catalog row 59. This page preserves catalog metadata; it does not establish that records were downloaded or their claims independently verified.

**Priority:** Useful

**Category:** Jobs & mobility

**Data source:** Mercatus Commuter Market Access Dataset for Congested Auto Travel (McMADCAT)

**Provider / steward:** Mercatus Center, George Mason University

**Provider type:** Research center / public data

**Geographic coverage:** 50 states and Washington, DC

**Granularity:** Census tract; five-minute travel-time bands

**Access / format:** Free ZIP downloads; summary and full data; PDF documentation

**Typical update:** Research release (2025 congested travel times)

**Primary hackathon uses:** Map jobs reachable from homes and workers reachable from job sites by congested car travel; compare housing sites and labor-market access

**Relevant problem briefs:** Typology & Climate; Observatory; Feasibility

**Likely join key:** Census tract GEOID

**Caveats / licensing notes:** Auto only; 60-minute cutoff and tract-centroid travel times. Mostly 2023 LODES jobs (Michigan 2021; Alaska 2016); 2019-2023 ACS. Free use with attribution requested.
