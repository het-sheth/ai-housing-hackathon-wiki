Catalog row 18. This page preserves catalog metadata; it does not establish that records were downloaded or their claims independently verified.

**Priority:** Core

**Category:** Demographics & housing

**Data source:** Decennial Census

**Provider / steward:** U.S. Census Bureau

**Provider type:** Public

**Geographic coverage:** United States

**Granularity:** Block to nation

**Access / format:** API and downloads

**Typical update:** Every 10 years

**Primary hackathon uses:** Population, households, occupancy, race/ethnicity, baseline small-area counts

**Relevant problem briefs:** Observatory; Typology & Climate

**Likely join key:** GEOID
