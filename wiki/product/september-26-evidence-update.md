# September 26 evidence update

## What changed

1. User-supplied SME feedback first emphasized zoning and financial feasibility, including comparable rents/sales and hard/soft costs. A later clarification emphasized that zoning and site constraints remain important and that doing one dimension well may outperform weak coverage of several. This is expert advice, not a judging rule or a validated customer workflow.
2. SMEs supplied the City code, map and zoning page and an ACTION-Housing presentation. Public links are below. Raw channel messages and attributed quotations are not reproduced here.
3. [Four presentation screenshots](/wiki/research/action-housing-presentation-excerpts.md) show budget and funding complexity and distinct participant objectives. The full talk remains unreviewed. These excerpts do not establish current costs or program eligibility.
4. [The Lanark worked example](/wiki/product/lanark-worked-example.md) demonstrates one exact parcel/assessment/permit match and full-polygon zoning/slope checks, with a meaningful source conflict. This supersedes earlier statements that no whole-parcel example exists; it does not establish region-wide coverage.
5. Rescope's public site advertises parcel-specific rules, source citations, overlays, map views and pipeline screening. A generic cited parcel brief overlaps substantially. Live performance and Pittsburgh availability remain unverified. We cannot claim the competitor is bad or lacks proposal comparison.

## Product implication, still a hypothesis

Recommend testing a comparison of two user-defined proposals on one parcel. Show which rule checks change, why they change and which unknowns persist. This addresses the question of what the user can change, but demand and market differentiation remain unproven. [Read the proposal for Het and Rushi](/wiki/product/proposal-comparison.md).
