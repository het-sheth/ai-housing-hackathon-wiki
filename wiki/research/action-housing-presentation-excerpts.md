# ACTION-Housing presentation excerpts

Four screenshots downloaded by Het and visually inspected on September 26, 2026 show slides 35, 36, 62 and 63 of a presentation playing in the video Building Affordable Housing by Action Housing. These are four excerpts of one presentation, not independent corroboration. The full talk, speaker explanation, original deck and publication date have not been verified. The SME described it as a presentation from two years earlier; that is user-supplied context.

The developer fee on slide 63 differs from developer fee plus overhead on slide 35; the screenshots do not establish whether this reflects different projects, categories or versions. Do not reconcile by guessing.

## Implications for the proposed product

These observations support keeping costs, capital sources, operating outcomes and housing objectives distinct. They do not show how staff initially screen sites, which software they use, or demand for our proposed tool.

Recommendation, not an accepted decision: defer a full pro forma and subsidy engine. A proposal comparison should preserve the user's housing objective and visibly report if a simpler option delivers fewer homes. Do not extrapolate per-unit costs or financing assumptions from these slides.
