## How the sources connect

Parcel ID links parcel attributes where compatible; polygon overlap links parcels with zoning or hazards; GEOID links Census/HUD measures at matching geography and vintage. Address matching is weaker and requires validation. Area statistics must not be presented as facts about a particular household.

## Starting data by track

1. Feasibility: parcel boundaries and assessments, zoning polygons and code, then selected physical/environmental flags. Permit data add context, not automatic approval probability.
2. Observatory: permits/sales plus ACS/CHAS and appropriately matched market aggregates. Choose a common geography and time basis.
3. Matchmaker: need indicators plus transit, land use and climate layers. State chosen weights and uncertainty.
