## Shared evidence, different decisions

The developer may avoid a site with expensive constraints. A nonprofit may pursue it because of a neighborhood or affordability mission. A planner may identify a review path or a possible intervention. An analyst needs consistent evidence over more sites before drawing policy conclusions. These are decision distinctions, not evidence of demand.

A proposed shared workflow is: declare a housing concept, inspect one parcel's relevant findings and uncertainties, identify the next review, and export the evidence. Comparing two or three sites for the same concept could reuse the same workflow if time and validated data allow. Portfolio statistics and intervention-effect estimates are deferred. We have not selected a primary user or approved this scope.

Read [persona comparison](persona-check-2026-09-26.md) and [competitor comparison](competitor-check-2026-09-26.md) for primary sources, dates and limits. Commercial capabilities are vendor claims. No hands-on comparative trial was conducted, and no claim that a competitor is bad or lacks an undocumented feature is justified.
