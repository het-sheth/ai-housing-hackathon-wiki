# Initial rule contract for the local build

Date: September 26, 2026. Research interpretation, not approved production logic or a parcel-specific legal determination. Based on the practitioner research workstream and current code links already inspected. Verify complete dependencies and version before implementation.

## Required inputs

Parcel ID and City jurisdiction; mapped district and rule version; current structure evidence and lawful existing use separately from County assessment; attached/detached form; current/proposed units; precise repair, expansion or reconstruction scope; footprint/height/coverage changes; known nonconformity; historic, slope/hillside and flood review status. Unknown is a valid input, not false.

## Conditional statements

1. For a lawfully established dwelling, maintenance/remodel/repair of a nonconforming structure can fall under section 921.03.A.1 if nonconformity does not increase. This is not an exemption from every permit or other review.
2. Expansion of a nonconforming structure requires a separate check under section 921.03.D.1 for compliance and non-increase of nonconformity. Plans and City interpretation may be necessary. Do not automatically conclude variance required or expansion forbidden.
3. Section 911.02 lists detached one-unit use as permitted in R1D. Attached classification involves section 911.04.A.69A and width. This is a use-table finding, not approval of a particular project.

## Minimum failure cases

1. Vacant-land assessment plus dwelling permit history: expose conflict, not absent building.
2. Unknown lawful use: no unconditional permission or variance verdict.
3. Added unit or expansion: no automatic same-use repair result.
4. Partial demolition or reconstruction: distinguish from repair.
5. Unknown attached form/width: no attached-use verdict.
6. Unchecked hazard/designation or stale code: preserve an unresolved check.
7. No permit record: no inference that a permit is unnecessary.
