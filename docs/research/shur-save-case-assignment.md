# Shur Save comparison research assignment

Contribution: Rushi Patel's September 26, 2026 discussion, consolidated September 27. This is a focused follow-up assignment, not evidence that its requested research has all been completed.

## Research question

What should a proposal-comparison brief show so a Pittsburgh housing practitioner can make a better next decision?

Start with the [November 7, 2023 WESA report](https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2023-11-07/development-plans-for-the-former-bloomfield-shursave-hit-a-snag). It describes a six-story, 248-apartment proposal, denied height/compatibility relief, and the developer's claim that the height was needed to cover costs. Treat the financial assertion as a party claim, not independently established feasibility.

Design hypothesis: fewer zoning obstacles may come with a loss of housing or other objectives. A comparison should preserve the tradeoff without declaring the smaller option feasible. Shur Save is a research case, not an automatic replacement for the small prototype demonstration.

## Copyable assignment

1. Follow reporting to original submitted plans, ZBA decisions, community documents and later revisions. Build a dated chronology. Separate what was known at each decision from later outcomes. Do not apply current code retrospectively without an appropriate historical baseline.
2. Extract housing and non-housing objectives, units, height, uses, requested approvals and dispositions. Preserve financial claims with speaker and date. Record failed retrievals and unknown fields.
3. Identify two documented versions, their status and whether they were actually simultaneous alternatives. If there is no documented alternative, list the inputs needed to define one. Do not manufacture a by-right design, yield or budget.
4. Identify the user and decision moment. Explain whether software could discover information, organize fragmented evidence, explain tradeoffs or merely restate existing professional analysis. Seek evidence against product usefulness.
5. Find one smaller Pittsburgh repair, expansion or infill case. Compare its evidence availability, decision value and implementation complexity with Shur Save. Keep distinct properties and multi-parcel projects separate until identifiers establish a relationship.
6. Specify a brief showing objectives, proposal versions, changed and persistent checks, housing outcomes, commitments, unknowns and next actions. Each action needs a role, document/request and reason.
7. Produce a sample brief and ask a reviewer what is misleading, who would use it and what action would change. Do not contact anyone automatically.

Return a source register, structured raw facts, chronology, documented comparison options, smaller-case recommendation, brief specification, sample brief and five unresolved datapoints ranked by design impact. Separate extraction from interpretation. Do not treat stakeholder support as independent feasibility evidence or the absence of a known competitor as uniqueness.
