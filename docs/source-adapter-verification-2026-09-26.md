## 2. Exact source contracts

All CKAN requests use `https://data.wprdc.org/api/3/action/datastore_search`. Require HTTP success, `success=true`, expected fields/types, exact returned keys and complete pagination before a complete-result claim. Retain provider resource ID as returned as well as configured alias. Set a 12-second timeout, 2 MB response ceiling, at most one retry for transient GET failures, and independent source results. Never accept a caller-supplied URL, SQL fragment, field list or geometry CRS.

ArcGIS adapters must also reject a response containing an application `error` despite HTTP 200, inspect `exceededTransferLimit`, and obtain complete results through a verified pagination or object-ID batching path before making coverage claims. Validate returned fields, CRS and all required geometries. Missing geometry, incomplete results or an unverified completeness path yields unresolved geographic coverage, never a complete-containment result. None of these failure/pagination paths was exercised by the successful sample queries.

## 3. Countywide identity algorithm, recommended

1. Preserve raw address/ID and normalize only whitespace/case and an explicit tested street-suffix dictionary. Treat parcel IDs as opaque strings, maximum 64 characters, allowing alphanumerics and separators. Do not impose the metadata's claimed 16-character length: observed canonical IDs here have 16 characters. Do not invent zero-padding rules. Human-readable map-block-lot can be resolved by exact boundary lookup, then canonical PIN confirmation, once that path is tested.
2. Search by exact PARID or parsed house number/street; keep fraction/suffix, municipality and ZIP visible. Use limit 21 to display at most 20 candidates and a refine-search prompt if truncated. ZIP narrows candidates only. Unknown street variants produce correction or parcel-ID entry, not a nearest-property substitution. Address parsing can suggest fields but cannot confirm a parcel.
3. User selects a candidate. Re-fetch exact assessment and exact boundary; require a single matching canonical key. Missing assessment or geometry remains a partial identity, with a correction/County verification task.
4. Query all municipal intersections with the full polygon. Use PostGIS geometry in EPSG:2272 to validate rings, holes and multipart geometry, then prove one municipality covers the parcel. Boundary-only contact with a neighbor is recorded; positive-area multi-municipality intersection or failed coverage remains unresolved. No centroid shortcut or silent snapping of slivers. Invalid geometry is not repaired silently.
5. Reconcile assessment and boundary municipality codes through a versioned authoritative crosswalk. Pittsburgh ward descriptions/codes map to municipality 100 only when the municipal polygon agrees. The sampled 125-to-100 match is verified; the complete ward crosswalk still needs verification. Non-City codes must likewise match, not merely names with similar spelling.
6. Return `confirmed`, `needs_selection`, `unresolved`, `outside_county` or `unavailable` plus observations. A failed address search alone cannot assert outside-county. Only confirmed City jurisdiction activates Pittsburgh checks. Multi-parcel assembly is retained as unsupported, with a task to establish the full site scope.

This approach avoids a paid geocoder and its licensing/billing dependency. Its tradeoff is less forgiving address entry. Address alias tests and boundary evidence determine whether it meets the accepted reliable countywide minimum. API samples alone do not establish that.

## 5. Rule-review limits and a real interpretation issue

The [nonconformities code](https://ecode360.com/45478965) includes certificate language in its preamble and section 921.06. The [City permit center](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Business-Development/Permits-Licenses-and-Inspections/OneStopPGH-Permit-Center) says single-family dwellings and townhouses do not require Certificates of Occupancy and warns against using assessment records to determine legal use. These sources cannot responsibly be reduced to a universal certificate-required boolean. Ask the City what evidence applies to the specific dwelling; disable any legal predicate that depends on an unresolved interpretation. This does not block the evidence/action demo.

The [use chapter](https://ecode360.com/45476515) is a candidate rule source, not proof of a reviewed rule edition. Pin section/version, dependencies, applicability, test examples, reviewer and reviewed-at date before enabling C07-C09 beyond conditional diligence questions. Review 922.02 and applicable definitions/overlays too; retrieval date is not effective date.

Current City search results describe a [Building and Development Application](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/Business-Development/Permits-Licenses-and-Inspections/Permitting/Building-Development-Application) replacing previous separate applications. Direct page opening failed in this research tool. Treat the changed terminology as a process-verification task, not a fully verified new routing engine. Repair versus addition may still share the same City next action. The accepted no-score product decision is not reopened by older organizer-score concerns.
