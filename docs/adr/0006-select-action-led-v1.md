# ADR 0006: Select an action-led v1 workflow

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-26
- Decision owner: Het and Rushi; approval by Het
- Approval evidence: Het accepted the six final interview choices: "agree with all. i have 10 dollars in openrouter balance + cursor".
- Supersedes: None. Narrows the interaction proposed in ADR 0005 without accepting its entire research scope.

## For Humans

### Decision

Make a proposal-specific assessment and prioritized next actions the primary result. Offer comparison when changing or duplicating a proposal. Use eight product pillars, named statuses and an evidence/action checklist, without an overall numeric ease score. Keep financial feasibility unassessed without necessary inputs.

Accept countywide intake, with countywide identity/assessment, verified permit coverage, a reviewed Pittsburgh subset and explicit gaps elsewhere as the launch minimum. Accept all work types without claiming all can be evaluated. Use a useful 2D site view; defer 3D. Preserve work and expose retry on AI failure.

### Why

Lanark can change reasoning without changing review status or the next action. Comparison should not manufacture decision value. An explicit next diligence task remains the product hypothesis to validate.

### Tradeoffs

Countywide intake exceeds the track's minimum geographic demonstration and increases source-resolution work. Bounded rule coverage limits automated answers. A checklist is less compact than a score but preserves blockers and unknowns.

## For Agents

### Context and evidence

See the consolidated specification and decision log for the accepted personas, personal projects, outcome-based tasks, version history and Supabase selection. The balances are user-reported $10 OpenRouter plus $75 Cursor. No extra purchases or automatic top-ups are authorized.

### Alternatives considered

Comparison as the default screen; a large ShurSave comparison as the main demo; a numeric overall score; 3D in v1. These were not selected for the current scope.

### Consequences and constraints

The scope is a design decision, not implemented coverage or practitioner validation. OpenRouter is a runtime candidate pending endpoint/model tests. Detailed architecture remains recommended, not blanket approved. Source rights and release verification remain prerequisites. Preserve source dates, contradictory records and financial unknowns.

### Revisit when

Practitioner testing shows the handoff does not change a useful next step, comparison demonstrates stronger value, or the launch minimum cannot be responsibly delivered.
