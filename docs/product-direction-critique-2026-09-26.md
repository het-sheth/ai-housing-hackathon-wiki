# Product direction critique

September 26, 2026. Brainstorming and product judgment only. Recommendations below are not approved scope or implementation authorization. Existing research and app work are preserved.

## Accepted workflow decision

Het explicitly accepted requiring a recorded task outcome before dependent work continues. Outcome recorded, evidence verified and requirement satisfied remain distinct. A negative or inconclusive outcome must produce an appropriate next action rather than automatic clearance. This is a product decision; persistence and task-resume functionality are not implemented.

## Recommendation for validation

Prioritize direction C: a proposal-aware Pittsburgh zoning and site screen with specific next checks and an exportable handoff. Retain A's comparison as optional, and use B's ShurSave record as a historical stress test rather than the primary claim of automated coverage.

Candidate decision: which diligence task and professional question should a project lead address next before committing further design resources? Candidate user is a small developer or nonprofit project lead after finding a site and forming an initial program, before commissioning detailed design. Report recipient is the relevant architect, surveyor, zoning professional or City contact. Neither persona demand nor time savings is established. Evidence does not support an automated acquisition or construction go/no-go recommendation.

A has lower implementation risk and existing Lanark evidence, but the default pair changes explanation only. B has stronger documented differences between requested approvals, but high domain scope, retrospective bias and missing verified alternatives. C has the best weekend chance of producing a defensible handoff, but substantial competitor overlap; it fails if it merely summarizes records or says contact the City.

The strongest objection to C is that it may collapse into another lookup report. Practitioner evidence of a changed diligence sequence, clarified professional question or avoided unsupported assumption is necessary before claiming value.

## Development Ease

Use proposal-specific statuses, evidence completeness and an action checklist. Do not calculate a composite number. Show the housing objective separately: retained/net new homes, affordability targets and any essential non-housing use. Do not let an option with zero new homes appear to win merely because it has fewer regulatory hurdles.

Separate known adverse decisions/constraints, documented required approvals, unresolved applicability and missing evidence. A favorable result for a narrow check is not overall clearance. Financial feasibility stays unassessed without credible development costs, operating/revenue assumptions, subsidy terms and financing assumptions. The organizer's invitation to devise a status system is not acceptance of this proposed presentation.

## Demo and validation limits

The current Lanark repair/expansion comparison is not a consequential decision demonstration: explanation changes while status and next action stay the same. The ground-disturbance contrast changes preparation from clarifying scope to reviewing disturbance plans, but remains a modest, practitioner-unvalidated task difference, not proof of a different permit path.

A stronger live comparison needs a concrete alternate plan, reliable dimensions/location, dated applicable rules and dependencies, and a verified change in the required evidence or review. The earlier approximately 190-unit ShurSave concept cannot be represented as an approved or viable fallback. The observed grocery-only outcome is a changed program with zero new homes, not a controlled design comparison or proof that denied variances caused the sale.

Keep comparison central only if a target practitioner can identify a consequential changed task, professional handoff, spend decision or selected proposal on a real paired case, using evidence available at that time. If the pair only changes wording, or the useful work is entirely shared between alternatives, make comparison secondary to the action-oriented screen. If that handoff also adds no value to existing practice, pivot to the specific unresolved workflow the practitioner identifies.
